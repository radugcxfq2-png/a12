About Rainwear

We read the hang tags so you can read the sky.

Rainwear is an independent site with one subject: jackets that keep rain out. We explain how they work, how to choose between them and how to keep one going.

Why we started

Rain jackets are sold with more numbers than almost any other garment, and very little explanation. People pay for ratings they do not need, or replace a jacket that only wanted a wash. A few clear pages can prevent both.

How we write

Guides draw on published test standards for water penetration and vapour transfer, on fabric and care-product makers’ own instructions, and on a good deal of time spent outdoors in poor weather. Each is reviewed by a second reader.

What we will not do

We do not sell jackets, link to shops for commission or accept payment for coverage. We do not test or rank individual products, and we never claim a jacket will keep you safe in dangerous conditions.
